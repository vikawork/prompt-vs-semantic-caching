Cloudreed support handbook

Cloudreed is an invented indoor household notice panel for this measurement. It is not an available product, and its policies do not describe a real business. The panel keeps shared tasks, ambient readings, and notices in one household account. It must not be used as an emergency alarm, a health monitor, or a dependable route for urgent messages. This handbook defines the fictional product's behavior and support policy. Answer from the relevant section, preserving conditions and exceptions. If the handbook does not establish a capability, say that it is unspecified rather than inventing an additional feature or a support promise.

1. Return eligibility

Hardware can be returned within forty-five days after delivery, whether opened or unopened, if the display and accessories are present and the unit has no customer-caused damage. Opening the box alone does not remove return eligibility. Remove the household from a working unit before packing it. If a fault prevents erasure, note that on the return request so the receiving process protects the stored data. A return request begins from the order page and needs a separate authorization. Canceling a subscription does not create a hardware return, and sending hardware back without authorization does not automatically cancel recurring billing.

2. Refunds and billing errors

A first subscription purchase can be refunded within fourteen calendar days of payment. Later renewal charges are not refundable merely because the household stopped using the panel. Duplicate charges and verified billing errors can be corrected regardless of that first-purchase window. Submit the invoice reference through Account, then Billing help, and choose the specific reason. Approved refunds go to the original payment method; they cannot be redirected to a new card. The refund history distinguishes approval, processor submission, and completion. A processor submission date is not a guaranteed bank posting date. Cancellation and refund requests are separate actions requiring separate confirmations.

3. Shipping a return

The customer arranges and pays for a change-of-mind return shipment. Support supplies a return authorization document, not a prepaid label. For a confirmed manufacturing fault or delivery damage, support supplies a prepaid label after reviewing the request. Each authorization names the approved order and remains valid for twenty-one days. Include the authorization document inside the parcel and keep the carrier receipt until the unit is recorded as received. A label issued for one unit cannot be reused for another unit. The receiving destination may change, so use the address on the current authorization rather than copying an address from an older shipment.

4. Warranty period

The hardware warranty lasts twelve months from delivery and covers manufacturing faults during normal indoor use. It excludes accidental damage, liquid exposure, unauthorized modifications, and failures caused by an incompatible power adapter. A replacement provided under warranty receives the unexpired part of the original warranty; it does not start a new full warranty period. The account's order record is acceptable proof of delivery date. Cloud service availability and subscription access are separate from hardware coverage. The warranty does not restore information lost after physical damage, and it does not make the panel suitable for emergency monitoring or outdoor installation.

5. Warranty and licence transfers

The hardware warranty belongs to the original purchasing household and does not transfer when a unit is sold or given away. A new household can still register compatible hardware after the previous owner removes it and erases local data. A personal software licence stays with the original account; it is not transferred with the unit. A team licence can be reassigned by a team administrator through the team console. Ownership transfer does not transfer cloud history, private tasks, or a subscription balance. The previous owner should verify both local erasure and account removal rather than assuming that unplugging the panel performs either action.

6. Power and the backup battery

Cloudreed uses the supplied nine-volt adapter and includes a backup battery for up to two hours of local display operation. The backup is intended for short interruptions, not everyday portable use. Wireless synchronization pauses when the panel switches to backup power. A battery symbol shows the remaining charge and a separate plug symbol confirms external power. Place the panel upright on a dry indoor surface with clear ventilation. Do not substitute an adapter with a similar connector. If a cable is damaged, a connector becomes unusually hot, or the case appears swollen, disconnect external power and stop using the panel until support responds.

7. Setting up a household

Setup requires a network connection and takes place on the panel's screen. Choose the language and time zone, verify the account email, and create an owner passphrase containing at least fourteen characters. The owner then selects whether the household wants shared tasks, private tasks, or both. Setup creates no paid subscription until the owner separately confirms a plan and a displayed charge. The recovery code appears at the end of account verification and should be stored outside the panel. It is not shown again. If verification cannot finish, setup pauses without erasing any information already entered in the setup form.

8. Supported networks

The panel supports both two-point-four-gigahertz and five-gigahertz password-protected wireless networks. A wired connection is available through the approved Ethernet adapter. Networks requiring a browser sign-in page and enterprise certificate authentication are not supported. Open Settings, then Network, to select or replace a connection. The connection screen distinguishes a wireless association failure from a service synchronization failure. Changing networks preserves local tasks and downloaded history. It does not remove the household account or update a payment card. If the service check fails while the local link passes, retry later before performing a reset that would remove useful local state.

9. Offline history

The panel keeps downloaded history and local tasks available offline for seven days. New local changes are queued, with their creation times preserved, until synchronization resumes. Remote changes made on another panel remain unavailable during the interruption. After seven days, shared remote-reading tiles are hidden, but the owner can still view locally created tasks. The screen displays the time of the last successful synchronization alongside any downloaded reading. A panel on backup battery also pauses synchronization even if the wireless network is working. Going offline does not pause billing, extend a warranty, cancel renewal, or submit a deletion request for the cloud archive.

10. Rebooting and erasing

Press the side button briefly, open the power menu, and choose Reboot to restart without deleting tasks or profiles. Holding the button for five seconds opens the recovery menu, which includes a factory-erasure option. Erasure requires the owner passphrase and confirmation of the displayed household identifier. It removes local accounts, tasks, connection settings, and downloaded history. It does not cancel paid service or delete the cloud archive. If the panel is unresponsive, disconnect external power and use the power menu's battery shutdown control when possible. Do not open the case or remove the battery as a troubleshooting step.

11. Export options

Version three exports task and reading history as a PDF report. CSV export is not supported. Choose Settings, then Records, then Create report, and select a connected removable drive. The report includes the chosen date range, time zone, units, and the creation time. Private task text is included only when the owner explicitly enables that option in the preview. A completed report is checked before the panel announces success. Removing the drive during creation can interrupt that check, so recreate the report if success was not shown. Printing a report does not remove the corresponding history from the panel or cloud archive.

12. Report date ranges

A single report covers no more than thirty consecutive calendar days. Start and end dates count toward that limit. For a longer period, create separate reports with adjacent date ranges and retain their original creation dates. The preview shows the earliest available record and prevents selection of an already deleted period. Existing records preserve the time zone in which they were created, and the report adds an ordering note when a selected period spans a time-zone change. The report generator cannot recover records removed by retention limits. Creating several overlapping reports is allowed, but overlap does not create additional history or extend its retention.

13. Profiles and owner powers

A panel supports one owner profile and three additional profiles, for four profiles in total. Shared tasks are visible to the household, while private tasks require the relevant profile passphrase. Only the owner changes the network, subscription, export settings, or archive deletion request. An additional profile can be renamed or removed without altering the remaining profiles. The owner cannot be removed while other profiles remain; first transfer ownership to an existing profile or erase the household. Transferring household ownership does not transfer the original purchaser's hardware warranty. The account page displays these two concepts separately so ownership and warranty coverage are not confused.

14. Temporary guest access

Guest access shows the clock and a selected shared task list. It hides private tasks, exports, billing records, and support attachments. The owner chooses which shared list is visible and sets an expiry of up to twelve hours. Guest access always expires automatically; there is no indefinite setting. A guest cannot edit the list or extend the expiry. Unlocking the full household requires the owner passphrase even if the guest session has not yet expired. Guest access limits what the normal screen shows, but it does not protect a panel from theft or physical tampering and is not a reason to leave recovery codes beside it.

15. Night mode

The default quiet interval is nine in the evening until six in the morning. Ordinary task sounds are muted and brightness drops during this interval. An urgent task may play a sound only if the owner separately enables Urgent sound override; that switch is off by default. Quiet times use the selected local time zone and can be previewed with the Test night mode button. Email delivery is governed by notification settings rather than this display interval. A time-zone change updates future quiet intervals without rewriting the recorded times of old tasks. Shared and private tasks follow the same quiet-hour rule on the physical panel.

16. Notification email

A weekly email digest is offered during setup and remains disabled until the destination address is verified. Immediate task emails are a separate optional switch. To stop all notification emails, disable both Weekly digest and Immediate notices under Account, then Notifications. Disabling the panel's sound does not disable either email option. A failed message is retried once and then marked failed in the notification history. That history masks the destination address on the shared display. A delivery failure does not mark a task complete or suspend the account. Changing the destination address requires verification again before any future notices are sent there.

17. Plans and plan changes

The standard plan covers one panel, four profiles, and fourteen days of synchronized history. The extended plan covers three panels and six months of synchronized history. Upgrades begin at the next monthly renewal; there is no immediate prorated upgrade. Downgrades also begin at renewal. The account page shows the scheduled plan and its start date, and the owner may change that schedule until the renewal is processed. Before a downgrade shortens history retention, the account sends an export reminder. Buying a replacement panel does not automatically add another paid plan, but the household must stay within the number of connected panels its active plan allows.

18. Subscription pauses

An extended subscription can be paused for three consecutive billing months, once per calendar year. The pause starts at the next renewal. Local tasks continue, but synchronized history is read-only during the pause and new remote updates wait until service restarts. The confirmation page shows the pause start and automatic restart dates before the owner accepts it. A one-month pause is not offered. An active pause cannot be shortened; the owner may cancel the scheduled restart instead if continued service is no longer wanted. Pausing service does not stop the hardware warranty clock or remove the need to keep a separate copy of important reports.

19. Canceling orders or renewal

Subscription cancellation is under Account, then Service, then Stop renewal. Paid access continues until the current period ends. Hardware order cancellation is under Account, then Orders, then Cancel order. Before dispatch, an order cancellation carries a fixed five-credit handling fee. Once dispatched, the order is not canceled and must use the return process after delivery. The confirmation screen identifies the action and any fee before approval. Deleting a profile, uninstalling a companion application, or resetting a panel does not stop renewal. Keep the cancellation receipt until the account shows the expected final service date or the order shows its final financial adjustment.

20. Billing dates and failed payment

Subscriptions are billed monthly on the first day of each calendar month. Annual billing is not available. The first purchase is charged only for the remaining days of that month, with the amount displayed before confirmation. A failed renewal payment gives a three-day grace period before remote service is suspended. Local tasks remain accessible. The owner can replace a saved payment card under Account, then Payments, and confirm a retry of the failed invoice. A new card does not erase the failed invoice or count as cancellation. Payment reminders do not ask for the panel passphrase, recovery code, or the full contents of a task report.

21. Spare parts and battery service

Replacement adapters and desk stands can be purchased through the parts page. A lost accessory is not covered by warranty. Battery replacement is a support service because the case is not designed to be opened by the household. Do not order a charger as a substitute for the specified adapter; the charging circuit is inside the panel and requires the approved power supply. Support may ask for the printed adapter part code and a diagnostic code when investigating unstable power. It will not ask for a passphrase or recovery code. A visibly damaged adapter or swollen case should remain out of use while the request is reviewed.

22. Getting help

Text support is available on weekdays from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon, in the account's selected local time zone. Phone support is not offered on either plan. Requests sent outside those hours receive a receipt and wait for the next opening period. The receipt identifies the request without displaying private task text. The queue is not an emergency service and does not continuously watch the panel. For a suspected electrical fault, disconnect power safely and stop using the unit instead of waiting for the queue. Changing plans does not move an existing request ahead of other requests already waiting.

23. Delivery estimates

Standard delivery is estimated at five to eight business days after dispatch. Payment confirmation and dispatch are separate events on the order page. Tracking appears after the carrier records its first parcel scan; a created label alone is not proof of collection. A delivery-date request made before dispatch is reviewed by the packing team and becomes binding only when accepted on the order page. After dispatch, date and route changes are handled through the carrier's available options. The support queue cannot guarantee an unoffered delivery slot. Orders placed on a nonbusiness day begin packing no earlier than the next business day.

24. Reporting delivery problems

Visible damage should be reported within ten days of receipt. Include a description, packaging photographs, and the affected part, and do not power on hardware that appears damaged. A missing parcel uses a different request category because there is no received unit to inspect. Check the carrier's recorded delivery location before opening that investigation. A replacement is not automatically dispatched merely because either form was submitted; support first confirms the order and investigation outcome. Neither form requires a household passphrase or a complete data report. Retain the damaged packaging until support confirms whether it is needed for collection or evidence.

25. Pickup and service areas

Collection is available in the invented service area Southport. Northport receives delivery only and has no pickup point. Select collection before completing payment, and wait for the Ready notice before going to the collection point. The notice provides a collection code distinct from the panel's recovery code. An authorized collector may use that collection code without receiving account access. Collection orders are held for seven days, after which the uncollected unit is returned to stock and the order is refunded after the stock check. Changing a delivery order into a collection order is possible only before dispatch and requires a new confirmation.

26. Software compatibility

Version two includes a PDF report feature for shared tasks. Version three adds private-task selection and improved time-zone notes to that report. Neither version exports CSV files. Compatible software updates are free and require owner confirmation before installation. The panel downloads the update, checks available space and external power, installs it, and restarts. Installation does not begin on backup battery alone. If the download fails, the installed version remains unchanged. Settings, then System details shows the current version and installation date. An update does not reset renewal dates, extend warranty coverage, or silently change the household's selected privacy settings.

27. Capacity and retention

The panel stores up to five thousand local history entries. When capacity is reached, the oldest completed tasks and readings are removed before any active task is considered. Active tasks remain until completed or explicitly removed. Standard-plan synchronized history lasts fourteen days, and extended-plan history lasts six months. A downloaded PDF report is a separate artifact unaffected by later history cleanup. The storage screen shows the remaining capacity and offers removal of completed local entries. Export useful records before cleaning them up. A low-capacity warning does not require a factory reset if ordinary deletion of completed history makes enough space available again.

28. Deleting the online archive

Archive deletion is requested under Account, then Data controls. Confirmation begins a seven-day recovery window. The owner may cancel the request during that window; after it closes, the cloud archive is permanently deleted and support cannot restore it. Local tasks on a disconnected panel remain until erased locally. Stopping renewal, deleting an additional profile, and removing a saved payment card are separate actions and do not initiate archive deletion. Create and retain any needed reports before the recovery window ends. A deletion confirmation states the archive scope and final deletion date so the owner can distinguish it from ordinary retention cleanup.

29. Cleaning and environment

Disconnect external power and switch the panel fully off before cleaning, because backup battery operation can keep it running after the cable is removed. Use a soft dry cloth, or a cloth slightly dampened with water for a stubborn mark. Keep moisture away from openings, never spray the case, and do not use abrasive cleaners. The panel is intended only for dry indoor rooms and is not suitable for outdoor use under a porch or awning. Let all surfaces dry before switching it on again. Cleaning does not require a reset, a new household, or a change to network credentials.

30. Understanding diagnostics

Code W1 means the network link is unavailable. Run the network test before changing account settings. Code H2 means history synchronization is waiting, while local tasks remain usable. Code B3 indicates a battery or power check requiring the unit to be switched off for inspection by support. A diagnostic package includes software version, error codes, and relevant event times. It excludes private task text unless the owner explicitly opts in after inspecting the package. Attach only the requested diagnostic package to a support case. Support does not need the account passphrase, recovery code, or an unrestricted archive export to investigate these diagnostic codes.
